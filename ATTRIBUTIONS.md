## Data sources

Service interval defaults compiled from publicly documented facts.
Trek maintenance schedule, Park Tool free articles, Shimano dealer
manuals, SRAM documentation, Stan's and Orange Seal tubeless sealant
documentation, Sheldon Brown Bicycle Technical Info, and community
consensus. Facts are not copyrightable (Feist v. Rural, 1991). The
service_intervals.json compilation is our own structured work.

Bike spec data for test fixtures from Cannondale official product pages.
